# 004. Synthesised sound effects instead of Kenney packs

Date: 2026-11-11
Status: accepted (Omar can reverse it after playtesting)

## Context
The Phase 2 spec says the sounds (place, clear, invalid drop, game over, new best) come from Kenney's CC0 interface/UI packs. CLAUDE.md allows code-drawn assets or CC0/OFL assets with credits.

## Decision
Generate the five effects in code with `apps/block_puzzle/tool/make_sfx.dart`. It writes small 16-bit mono 22.05 kHz WAVs to `assets/audio/`:
- place: short falling "tock" with a tiny click.
- clear: two-note chime (root plus a fifth). The game raises its playback rate by `kClearPitchStep` per streak step, up to `kClearPitchMax`.
- invalid: two soft low pulses.
- game over: three falling notes.
- new best: a rising four-note arpeggio.

## Consequences
- No third-party audio files, so there are no licences or credits to track for sound, and the sounds are original by construction.
- The sounds are easy to tune: change the generator and re-run it. The output is committed so builds don't depend on running the tool.
- They will sound simpler than a professional pack. If Omar's playtest feedback (Phase 4 acceptance) says so, switch to Kenney CC0 files. That only changes the file names in `lib/src/game/sfx.dart` and adds a credits entry.

## Addendum (2026-11-11): menu sounds
At Omar's request, two subtle menu sounds were added, generated by the same tool: `ui_tap` (a 35 ms soft high tick) and `ui_toggle` (two tiny rising blips, played at 1.12x for on and 0.9x for off). Volumes are 0.35 and 0.4 (`constants.dart`), well under gameplay sounds. `game_core` has a `UiSounds` hook that `GameButton` plays through, so every game's menus can use it. The block puzzle connects it to its audio service, so the Sound effects setting covers menu sounds too.

## Addendum (2026-11-11): one consistent sound family
Omar: "The sound effects in game are inconsistent and the one where the row is taken out is quite piercing."

- **Cause.** Each sound used a different voice (sine knock, bright 880/1320/1760 Hz chime, buzzy pulses, triangle melody, sine arpeggio) at uneven loudness. The streak pitch-up raised the clear chime up to 1.6x, so its harmonics reached about 2.8 kHz, the harshest band on a phone speaker.
- **Now.** Every sound uses one voice, a soft mallet (fundamental plus a quiet, fast-fading 2x overtone, 4 ms attack), with notes from C major pentatonic:
  - place: one short C4 knock;
  - clear: a quick C4–E4–G4–C5 strum;
  - invalid: soft falling D4–A3;
  - game over: slow G4–E4–C4;
  - new best: rising C4–E5;
  - menu: short A4 tick and a G4–C5 toggle.
- **Loudness.** Each file is peak-normalised to a per-sound level (0.45–0.6).
- **Clear pitch with streak.** It now steps up musical intervals (`kClearStreakSemitones` = 0, 2, 4, 5, 7 semitones; max 1.5x) via `clearRate()`, instead of +6% per step up to 1.6x. The highest frequency played is now about 1.6 kHz.

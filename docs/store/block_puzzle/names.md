- Not done: a formal trademark search. Before committing, search IP Australia (ATMOSS) and USPTO (TESS/Trademark Center) for the chosen name in class 9 (software) and class 41 (games).

## Recommendation
1. **Lantern Blocks.** Warm, easy to say and remember, no clashes found, and it fits the look: dark board, glowing blocks, the pulsing clear preview. A lantern also makes a simple, original icon.
2. **Sundial Blocks** (runner-up). Ties naturally to the Daily challenge and streaks ("a new puzzle every day"), and no clashes were found.
